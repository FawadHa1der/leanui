Verify a roster

A receipt is a proof, written by Lean, that a roster staffs a problem: everyone works at most one shift, and only one they can do, and every shift gets the people it needs. This page checks it again, in your browser, with nanoda, a Lean kernel written apart from Lean's own (in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly). Receipts come from the Roster app's Notarize button.

or drop one on this page.

    Changes the receipt's data, not its proof: someone the roster puts on a shift can no longer do it. The receipt must then be rejected.

    What was proved

    The theorem Roster.Receipt.valid states Roster.Problem.Valid problem roster for the problem and roster above. Valid p r says three things about the roster r, which gives each person the shift they work, if any (so nobody works two): it has one entry per person; whoever works shift s can do it (canWork p i s: row i, column s is ticked); and every shift gets at least the people it needs (needOf p s), counted with List.count. The proof is a computation: the kernel runs the checker checkRoster on the data, gets true, and checkRoster_iff turns that into Valid.
